About Institute,
Dr. D. Y. Patil School of Design

Established in 2022, DPU | Dr. D. Y. Patil School of Design enriches the legacy of Dr. D. Y. Patil Vidyapeeth, Pune, with a focus on exceptional design education. Our programs empower students to creatively solve real-world design challenges by blending innovation, technical skill, and design empathy. We offer an AICTE-approved Bachelor of Design with specializations in Fashion Design, Communication Design, Interior Spatial & Furniture, Product Design, UX Design and Animation & VFX Design. Supported by cutting-edge facilities and guided by experts from premier institutes like NID and IIT, our faculty fosters a dynamic learning environment shaping the institute as best design institutes across India. DPU SOD strives to be a leading hub for design innovation and education, nurturing the next generation of design leaders in India.

About University,
Dr. D. Y. Patil Vidyapeeth

The beginning in 2000, Dr. D. Y. Patil Vidyapeeth, Pimpri, Pune (DPU) was established under the leadership of the great visionary and philanthropist, Dr. D. Y. Patil Sir.
Dr. D. Y. Patil Vidyapeeth, Pimpri, Pune is a private, non-profit, self-financing organization, dedicated to serving society and making a difference. Initially, it had a humble beginning with only one institute under its fold i.e. Dr. D. Y. Patil Medical, College & Research Centre. The Institute started as a Centre of Excellence with emphasis on medical education, research and healthcare. Within a short span of time, the institute gained recognition as one of the best in the State and across the country. Gradually, over the last 20 years, there has seen a humongous growth and 14 more professional institutes have been added under its umbrella.

About, Eros AIVidya

Today, we stand at the beginning of the most significant transformation yet: the age of Artificial Intelligence.
AI is not replacing creativity; it is expanding it. It is giving students, creators and entrepreneurs the ability to imagine, build and distribute content at a scale that was previously impossible. The future creative professional will combine artistic vision with AI-native skills, working across film, music, animation, gaming, storytelling and entirely new forms of expression that are only now emerging.
That is the vision behind Eros AIVidya.
Built on nearly fifty years of filmmaking experience and cultural storytelling, Eros AIVidya combines the heritage of one of the world’s leading entertainment groups with the future of AI-powered creation. Drawing upon decades of engagement with the global creative and academic community, including my experience serving on the Board of Advisors of the UCLA School of Theater, Film and Television, we have designed what we believe is a first-of-its-kind learning ecosystem for the AI era. This is not simply a programme about AI. It is a platform built around it.
Students will learn from the world’s best knowledge resources while gaining access to practical, industry-focused pathways across AI, filmmaking, music, storytelling, animation, creator economy, entrepreneurship, wellness and emerging technologies. They will not simply study creativity; they will create. They will not simply learn about industries; they will participate in them.
For most of history, access to opportunity depended on geography, resources or connections. Today, a student with imagination, determination and the right tools can create, collaborate and compete on a global stage from anywhere in the world.
I believe the defining creative talent of the twenty-first century will emerge not only from Hollywood, Silicon Valley or traditional centres of excellence, but from our universities, our cities, our towns and our communities. Our responsibility is to provide the infrastructure, mentorship and opportunities that allow that talent to flourish.
The future belongs to those who can learn continuously, think creatively, adapt rapidly and build responsibly. Education must evolve accordingly.
To every student beginning this journey: you are not simply learning to use technology. You are learning to build worlds.

Programme Details

Bachelor of Films and Creative Media

Step into the world where stories inspire, ideas come to life, and creativity shapes the future. The Bachelor of Films & Creative Media is a dynamic 4-year degree programme designed for aspiring filmmakers, storytellers, content creators, and media professionals who want to build impactful careers in the ever-evolving creative industry.

Offered in collaboration with EROS INNOVATION LTD., this programme blends academic learning with practical industry exposure, empowering students to develop creative, technical, and professional skills required in today's media landscape.

The UK Programme

Eros Innovation is establishing a major UK-based programme combining film and creative investment with sovereign, rights-led Cultural AI infrastructure. We are committing £250–265 million over five years into UK film production, content development and AI capability, anchored by the development of Large Cultural Models (LCMs) built on rights-cleared datasets.
The programme is underpinned by a cultural dataset of approximately 1.5 trillion licensed tokens, independently valued at $1.7 billion by OxValue (associated with the University of Oxford) — believed to be one of the largest rights-cleared cultural datasets globally.
This is a production-scale programme, already recognised within the Government of India's AI ecosystem, now being extended to position the UK as a global leader in ethical, rights-aware AI.
